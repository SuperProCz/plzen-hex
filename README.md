# Plzeň Hex

**Explore the Plzeň Region one hexagon at a time.**

👉 **Play now:** https://92-5-185-123.sslip.io/
📊 **Where people travel:** https://92-5-185-123.sslip.io/dashboard/

Plzeň Hex turns a trip around Plzeňský kraj into a game. The whole region is covered by a map of hexagons. Go somewhere new and the hexagon you're standing in unlocks, along with the castles, churches, lookout towers and villages inside it. The more you explore, the more of the region you collect.

## Features

- **Unlock the map by visiting.** Your phone's location unlocks the hexagon you're in. Around towns with lots of sights, hexagons split into smaller pieces so there's more to discover.
- **1,700 monuments to find.** Castles, chateaux, monasteries, churches, technical monuments and more. They stay grey until you discover them, and most have a photo and a Wikipedia link.
- **Nature too.** 49 lookout towers and over 1,200 named peaks.
- **Badges with bronze, silver and gold.** One for every kind of place: towns, villages, districts, castles, chateaux, peaks and so on. Each level comes with a title, from "Hradní panoš" to "Hradní pán".
- **Levels and XP.** Every discovery earns points, from "Nováček" all the way to "Legenda Plzeňska".
- **Milestones.** Reach the region's northernmost, southernmost, westernmost and easternmost points, climb its highest peak, visit the Prazdroj brewery, keep a 30-day streak and more.
- **Daily challenges.** Three new challenges every day, the same for everyone.
- **Trip journal.** A diary of everything you've discovered and when.
- **Nearest goal.** The map always points you to the next castle, tower or milestone nearby.
- **Celebrations.** Pop-ups, confetti and sound effects whenever you earn something.
- **Works on the go.** Use it in the browser or as an Android app. Your progress keeps counting even without signal.
- **Help for the region.** The app counts how often each hexagon is visited, completely anonymously, with no accounts, routes or personal data. The public dashboard shows a live heatmap of where people travel, which can help the region plan tourism. Players can switch this off.
